Splash concepts, round 2

Five generative pieces, each built up by the five boot steps, resolving at ready and stalling gracefully on failure. Thumbnails are frozen at the Ready moment; open one for theme, accent, Replay and Fail controls.

AssemblyBauhaus primitives drift out of the margins and lock into a precise composition, a few per step; ready snaps it to the grid.
InkOne drop of pigment per step diffuses into still water; five drops settle into a balanced, watercolour composition.
PrismA single beam meets a hairline prism; each step lets another band of the spectrum out, opening the fan around the accent.
FormulaKaava made visible: a two-line attractor developed like a photographic print, five exposures out of the dark.
AccretionA curling, frost-like structure grows outward ring by ring, one ring per step; a wave of light crosses it at ready.
Query params on every prototype: ?freeze=SECONDS & fail=SECONDS & theme=light & accent=amber|blue|none & seed=N & reduce=1 (still-but-progressive).
